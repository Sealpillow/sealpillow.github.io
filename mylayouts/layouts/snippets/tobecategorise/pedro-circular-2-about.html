PedroCircular

A research platform exploring the life, meaning and future of what already exists.

Why it Exists

Waste is an inevitable part of making. Every pattern cut leaves behind pieces that no longer belong to the original design. Their irregular proportions often make them difficult to use again, despite the quality of the material remaining unchanged.

Project Circular begins at this point. Rather than viewing these remnants as the end of one process, we explore how they might become the beginning of another. Guided by the material's size, shape and character, design unfolds differently. Constraints become prompts, and familiar limitations become opportunities for new forms of creativity.
