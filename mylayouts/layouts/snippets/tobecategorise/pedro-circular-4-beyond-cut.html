Beyond the Cut

Every exploration begins by looking again. Rather than working towards a predetermined outcome, curiosity takes precedence over certainty, allowing ideas to evolve through experimentation and continual reflection. The process remains open enough for unexpected directions to emerge, where each discovery reshapes the next.

Every fragment, surface, and offcut carries the potential for another expression, another purpose, another beginning. Through this process of reconsideration, possibilities emerge that have always existed beneath the familiar.

Not every exploration is expected to arrive at an answer. Some uncover new processes, and sometimes simply a new question worth pursuing. Each outcome becomes part of an evolving body of research, creating a foundation for future ideas, collaborations, and expressions of design that continue to grow over time.
