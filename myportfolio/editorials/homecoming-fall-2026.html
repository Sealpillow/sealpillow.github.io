Homecoming


Fall 2026

Fall 2026

Some places remain with us long after we've left them. They reveal themselves in familiar routines, in the character of lived spaces and in the objects that accompany us through each day. For Fall 2026, PEDRO reflects on these enduring traces, considering how a sense of home can influence the way we see, dress and live.

A restrained palette draws from domestic interiors, while tactile surfaces and balanced proportions lend each piece a sense of ease. Designed to be lived with rather than simply worn, every detail reveals itself gradually over time.

Home, perhaps, is less a place than a feeling carried forward; found in the pieces reached for without hesitation, time after time. The collection is an invitation to return, not to where we began, but to the enduring comfort of what continues to feel true.
