Every day with PEDRO

Inside The Everyday Lives Of Style Makers

For 20 years, PEDRO has explored the relationship between design, materials, and everyday life, creating lasting style through thoughtful details. To celebrate its 20th anniversary, PEDRO presents “Every day with PEDRO,” featuring florist Cheng Fan-Cheng, hairstylist Eddy, and furniture curator Dan. Each brings their own perspective to the new Hybrix suede loafers for Fall/Winter.

Different professions, different rhythms of life, yet one shared belief: true style is shaped through the choices we make every day.

Staying true to yourself in every role
Florist Cheng

A florist, fashion KOL, singer, and actor, Cheng moves effortlessly between different roles, drawing inspiration from everyday life and turning subtle observations into creative expression.

Moving between floral creations and different work settings, Cheng wears the taupe Hybrix Suede Loafers. Soft suede and a comfortable platform sole strike a balance between polished and casual, reflecting his effortless lifestyle.

“I don’t have to be just one version of myself. Every side of me is who I am.” — Cheng

The right balance lies in the smallest details
Hairstylist Eddy

For hairstylist Eddy, every detail matters—from hair flow and proportions to personal features. Finding the right balance is at the heart of his work, much like PEDRO’s approach to craftsmanship.

For long days on his feet, Eddy chooses the taupe Hybrix Suede Loafers, combining vintage-inspired suede with a comfortable platform sole for effortless comfort and a refined finish.

The Vienna Suede Tote Bag offers a spacious, unisex silhouette for work and everyday essentials, while its warm tone adds subtle texture to his Fall/Winter style.

“I just try to get every detail right, because a tiny change can help someone start over.” — Eddy

Discovering the timeless value of craftsmanship
Furniture curator, Dan

As the founder of a furniture curation business, Dan believes true taste comes from thoughtful choices in materials, structure, and quality—not from following trends.

Moving between showrooms and traveling in search of unique pieces, Dan wears the dark brown Hybrix Suede Loafers, combining refined leather and warm suede for an understated look that works across formal and casual settings.

“The traces left behind give each object its own character.
And style, too, takes shape through the choices we make every day.” — Dan

Style, made for every day.

Three professions, three ways of life, and three perspectives on personal style. From creativity and work to travel, PEDRO brings together materials, craftsmanship, and functionality for every part of everyday life.

Find your own rhythm through the choices you make every day. Every day with Pedro.
