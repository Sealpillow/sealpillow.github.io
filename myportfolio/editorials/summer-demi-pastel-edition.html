THE DEMI

Pastel Edition

An evolution of a classic, the Demi returns in a palette of pastel hues that introduce a lighter, more playful mood. These soft washes of colour bring a gentle brightness to a familiar form, lending the silhouette a sense of modern femininity while preserving the clarity of its original design. Recognizable yet refreshed, the collection carries a simple idea at the heart of the season: ease, warmth, and the understated pleasures of summer.

The iconic curved silhouette remains the defining element of the bag, shaped in our signature Grano leather. Selected for its supple texture and enduring structure, the material is a tactile anchor that softens beautifully with wear. It is a refined reinterpretation of a brand favourite, designed to balance a soft aesthetic with the resilience required for seasonal movement.
