The Emblem

The emblem is more than a symbol;
it is the distilled expression of our
ethos of connecting the wearer to
the brand’s commitment to self-
expression and infinite possibility. It
is the intersection where modern
design meets lasting craft.

Meticulously balanced to signify equality and modern luxury, prominent parallel lines
represent a constantly evolving brand that corresponds directly with its community,
while a subtly placed infinity symbol expresses limitless creative possibilities
for the future.
