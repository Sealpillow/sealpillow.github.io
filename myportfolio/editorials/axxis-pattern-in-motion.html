Created to commemorate PEDRO’s twentieth anniversary, AXXIS translates two decades of design into a distinctive visual language. A geometric print unfolds through continuous repetition, bringing a sense of cohesion to the collection.​

Drawing on the idea of continuity, the pattern extends ad infinitum across each silhouette. Shifting through different forms, scales, proportions and placements, it takes on new expressions while retaining a recognisable visual character.
