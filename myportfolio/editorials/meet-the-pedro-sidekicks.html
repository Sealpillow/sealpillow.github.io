A New Winter Favourite Arrives

Meet The
PEDRO Sidekicks

Winter has a way of making the world feel smaller and a habit of reshuffling moods.

The PEDRO Sidekicks return this winter, reimagined and ready to charm. This four-part series

own narrative, because even your holiday décor should have a story to tell.

This is retail therapy with lore.

Cloud The Dreamer

Cloud drifts through life as if the world were one long, half-written story. Their mind is always elsewhere, and logic comes second if at all. Schedules exist only as suggestions, and they float in and out of moments, leaving traces of inspiration behind. People chase results, but Cloud inspires imagination, a fleeting spark that lingers long after they have moved on.

Marble The Mastermind

Marble has a talent for turning chaos into opportunity. Ideas spill from their notebooks as quickly as coffee spills from their cup, forming plans that somehow make sense only to them.
