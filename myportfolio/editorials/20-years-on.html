A Point of View
From the Beginning

PEDRO began in 2006 with a desire to bring a different perspective to everyday dressing. From the beginning, the emphasis was on pieces made with a little more thought, balancing fluid forms with details that gave them a distinct character. As the brand grew, this approach has continued to develop alongside the way we dress and live. The collections have changed over the years, but the interest in making the familiar feel new remains.

( 2006 - 2026 )

The way a piece looks often begins with what it is made from. Materials are chosen for the qualities they bring to a design, and the way they can change the character of a silhouette. As textiles and materials have expanded through the years, we continue to explore different ways of working with them, bringing different techniques into the process while keeping the focus on how the finished piece feels and wears.

Demi Bag

Asher Tote in Grano Leather

As PEDRO has moved into new cities and markets, stores have become places to experience the brand beyond the collections themselves. Collaborations have offered other ways of bringing people together, while working with a community of tastemakers has brought different perspectives along the way. These encounters have become part of the story, shaping the brand as much as the places it has grown into.

Cambodia Sihanouk Store in 2014.

Cambodia Sihanouk Store in 2025.

Singapore Wisma Atria PEDRO Store in 2026.

There is still much to explore. The way we live continues to change, as do the materials we work with and the places we inhabit. We continue to look to these shifts for inspiration, bringing what we have learned into future collections and ways of working. The next chapter is not about leaving the past behind, but seeing where the same curiosity can take us next.
