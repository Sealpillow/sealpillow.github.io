What happens if my delivery address is incorrect or if the delivery fails?

Once shipped, we are unable to change the delivery address. If the address is incorrect, the parcel may be returned and you will be responsible for both the original and return shipping charges.

Please double-check your address before placing an order, especially the country field (e.g., Austria vs Australia). To update saved addresses, log in and edit them in your Address Book.

If no one is available at the time of delivery, the courier will attempt to re-deliver the parcel once more.

Where does Pedro ship and are there any restricted areas?

PEDRO ships from Singapore to selected countries in Asia, Europe, the Middle East, North America, and Oceania.

For Cambodia and Pakistan, please shop via their respective local online stores. Deliveries must be made to a local address corresponding to the destination country.

We do not ship to PO boxes, military, or protected areas. In Singapore, we are unable to deliver to certain restricted areas such as Changi Airport, Jurong Island, MINDEF COMCEN, and PSA Terminal Wharves (Tanjong Pagar, Pasir Panjang, Sembawang).

Deliveries to hotels are accepted but only to the reception. PEDRO is not responsible for any loss after delivery is accepted by hotel staff.

Are duties, taxes and tax refunds applicable to my Pedroshoes.com order?

All Singapore deliveries include local sales tax, which is automatically applied at checkout in accordance with Singapore regulations. You will not incur additional duties, taxes, or hidden charges upon delivery.

Tax refunds are not available for purchases made on PEDROSHOES.COM.

How do I take care of my PEDRO products?

Our products are made of genuine leather; synthetic fabrics and materials; and metallic accessories which can react to humidity, salt and atmospheric agents. This may cause irreversible wear and tear, so we recommend you to keep your item(s) dry.

Overexposure to light and heat; direct contact with rain; oil and grease (makeup); alcohol (perfume, solvents); and other abrasive products or excessive friction, should be avoided as colour fading may occur. Care should be taken when wearing lightly-coloured garments and/or when storing products with different colours together as the colours may transfer onto each other.

To keep your shoes, bags and accessories clean, wipe them with a soft dry cloth. When they are not in use, store them in a protective bag in a clean, cool and dry place.

All our materials are carefully crafted and sourced for their unique qualities. Any incidental marks, tonal changes and/or textural variances are part of the material’s natural characteristic and should not be considered as imperfections.

What is Payment Authorisation?

Payment authorisation is confirmation of a cashless payment given during a payment transaction. It may involve the customer entering a PIN in a card transaction. All payments are authorised and captured once your order has been placed.

Why do I see double records on my online bank statement for my purchase?

All payments made using a debit card will result in two records: authorisation and settlement. You will see both records reflected on your online bank statement. Authorisation refers to funds held by the bank while the payment is in the process of being approved or declined. Settlement refers to the actual payment (i.e the merchant has captured the fund that was on hold previously).

Why are there extra charges reflected on my credit card bill?
