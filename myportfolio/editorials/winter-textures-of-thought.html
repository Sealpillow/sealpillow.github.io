Textures of Thought

In an intimate exploration of texture and form, PEDRO turns its gaze inward, unveiling the brand’s signature Grano leather. Framed by winter hues and tactile accents, from faux shearling on bags to horsebit hardware on footwear, the narrative deepens in texture and tone. Defined by its subtle grain and presented through refined winter silhouettes, the material finds expression in softened structures and sculptural shapes that move with quiet assurance. Each piece a balance between touch and design, where the essence of leather becomes part of the season’s rhythm.

The Mood of Design​


Shot through an introspective lens, the editorial reveals not just the leather itself but the mood it evokes. Gestures unfold with quiet ease, with silhouettes inviting discovery. Crafted for those who find meaning in detail, value in restraint, and expression in form. The kind of individual who moves with quiet confidence, guided by the belief that true refinement lies not in what draws attention, but in what endures.
