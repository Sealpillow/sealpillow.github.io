PEDRO OPENS
AT GRAND INDONESIA


Grand Indonesia, one of Jakarta’s most prominent
retail landmarks, draws both domestic shoppers and
international visitors to its expansive corridors. As
PEDRO’s latest store opens in this iconic mall, it
stakes its claim through thoughtful design, material
richness, and spatial clarity.

The store presents the brand’s complete offering: footwear, bags, and leather
goods. Conceived not simply as a point of sale, but as a platform for discovery,
PEDRO distinguishes itself through a combination of spatial clarity, material
richness, and a carefully curated palette.

The store presents the brand’s complete
offering: footwear, bags, and leather goods.

Materiality defines the experience from the moment one enters. White wood with a
black patina appears in accents throughout, introducing a new nuance to the
brand’s design language. Stainless steel adds an industrial counterpoint to textured
plaster walls and stone coatings, while circular lines and continuous forms are
threaded throughout the layout, echoing ideas of renewal, reflection, and continuity.

Spatial strategy extends to the way products are showcased. A dedicated shoe area
orbits communal seating, providing a reflective space for visitors or a quiet moment
to try on footwear. Accessories are framed by their surroundings to allow form and
detail to emerge clearly. All these serve the dual purpose of delighting the shopper
while reinforcing brand identity.
